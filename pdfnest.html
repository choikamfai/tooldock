Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every conversion happens on your own device using your browser. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tools will still work.

Is ToolDock really free?

Yes. All 100 tools are free with no sign-up, no usage limits and no watermarks added to your files.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no limit set by ToolDock. The practical limit is your device's memory — very large files (hundreds of MB) work best on a computer rather than a phone.

Can I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone?

Yes. Use the HEIC to JPG tool: add one or more .heic photos and download standard JPGs that open anywhere.

How do I password-protect a PDF?

Use the Protect PDF tool: choose a password and the file is encrypted with AES-256, right in your browser. It will ask for that password every time it is opened. There is no way to recover a lost password, so keep it somewhere safe.

Can you remove the password from a PDF?

Yes, with the Unlock PDF tool — if the file asks for a password to open, enter it and you will get an unlocked copy. It cannot crack or guess passwords; you need to know the current one.

Why is there no PDF to Word, Word to PDF or PDF to Excel?

Converting between PDF and Office formats properly — keeping the original layout, tables, columns and fonts intact — cannot be done well inside a browser. A browser-only version can only pull out the plain text and rebuild a rough document, which loses most of the formatting; we would rather not ship a half-finished tool. ToolDock’s PDF shelf launches with the seventeen tools that work completely on your device, and full Office conversion is planned for a later server-based version, where a real conversion engine can do it justice.

Can I sign a PDF here?

Yes. With Sign PDF you draw your signature with mouse, finger or stylus, type your name, or upload a photo of your signature, then click on the page to place it. It is a signature image placed on the page — convenient for everyday forms, but not a cryptographic digital signature (e-signature certificate).

How does HTML to PDF handle web pages?

Paste HTML code or drop an .html file and it is rendered in your browser and saved as an A4 PDF. Scripts in the HTML are not run, and images hosted on other sites may be blocked by their CORS policy, so a downloaded page may not look pixel-perfect.

How much smaller will Compress make my PDF?

It depends on the file. Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs often shrink by half or more in Balanced or Strong mode, which rebuild pages as images. Text-based PDFs shrink less; Light mode keeps text selectable and only optimizes the file structure.

About ToolDock

ToolDock is a free online toolbox of 100 tools for everyday file, text and number jobs: 17 PDF tools (merge, split, compress, rotate, protect, sign and convert), developer utilities such as a JSON formatter and Base64 encoder, calculators for mortgages, calories, BMI and GPA, image tools, unit converters, text and color tools, a QR code generator, a barcode generator and timers.

What makes ToolDock different from most tool sites is where the work happens. Every tool runs locally in your browser with JavaScript — your files are never uploaded to a server. Contracts, ID scans, statements and family photos stay on your own device, there are no accounts to create, and there are no daily quotas or watermarks on your results.

The scope is deliberately honest. The tools do the jobs that can be done well on-device; full Office conversion (PDF to Word, Word to PDF, PDF to Excel) is not included, because a browser-only conversion loses the original layout, tables and fonts — it is planned for a later server-based version instead of shipping a half-finished tool. Each tool page explains what the tool does, how to use it, and the limits that apply.

ToolDock is an independent project and is free to use. Because processing power and memory come from your own device, a very large file that struggles on a phone will usually succeed on a computer — that is the one trade-off of never uploading anything.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Your files and inputs stay on your device

Every ToolDock tool processes files and text locally in your browser. We operate no upload endpoint for tool content, and none of your files, photos, documents, pasted text, passwords or calculator inputs are sent to, received by, or stored on our servers. When you close the page, the data your browser was holding for the tool is gone with it.

Information we collect

ToolDock has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information in order to use the tools. We do not run analytics that record what you type into a tool or which files you open.

Like any website, ToolDock is delivered over the internet: the hosting provider that serves the site and the content delivery network (jsDelivr) that serves the open-source code libraries the tools rely on may record standard technical data — such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested — in their server logs, under their own privacy policies. Downloading the page at all creates those ordinary web requests; using a tool afterwards creates no further requests about your content.

Cookies

ToolDock itself does not set cookies for tracking or advertising. If third-party services such as advertising are added to the site in the future, they may set their own cookies, and this policy will be updated to describe them before they are introduced.

Children

The tools are general-audience utilities. Since the site collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

Signatures and barcodes

Sign PDF places a signature image on a page; it is not a certified electronic signature and may not satisfy legal requirements that call for one. The Barcode Generator draws valid EAN-13, UPC-A and Code 128 barcodes, but generating an EAN or UPC number here does not register it — retail use normally requires a number range issued to you by GS1.

Do not use the tools to break the law — including removing protection from documents you have no right to open, forging documents or signatures, or infringing someone else's rights.
